What this website uses

Necessary: your privacy choice

When you choose whether to load third-party content, we save that choice in your browser’s local storage (key amari.consent) for up to 12 months. It contains no data that identifies you and is not sent to any server.

Optional: Google Maps

The location map is a Google service. It only loads if you click “View map” (we remember that choice) or accept it in your preferences. When it loads, Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, under Google’s privacy policy.

Typeface

The typefaces (Karla and IvyMode) are loaded from this website itself, without connecting to third-party services.

Links to other websites

Links to the booking engine (Cloudbeds), WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube and Google Maps open third-party websites, which apply their own cookie policies.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How to change your preferences

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the “Cookie preferences” button at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings.
